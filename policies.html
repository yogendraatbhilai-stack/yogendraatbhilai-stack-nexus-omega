Omega Financial — Code of Conduct

This code governs everyone's use of Nexus and the client relationships it supports. It exists to protect clients, the firm, and everyone who works here.

1. Client ownership

Every client serviced through Nexus is a client of Omega Financial, regardless of who the relationship manager is or how the client was originally introduced. No employee may service a Nexus client under a personal ARN, a side business, or any external distributor code.

2. Confidentiality

Client data, portfolio details, contact information, and investment recommendations accessed through Nexus are confidential. They may not be shared, exported, or discussed outside Omega Financial for any purpose, including after an employee's departure from the firm.

3. Intellectual property

The calculators, methodologies, portfolio models, and reporting formats inside Nexus are the intellectual property of Omega Financial. Reproducing, copying, or reusing them for personal or third-party benefit is a violation of this code.

4. Accountability

Every report generated through Nexus is watermarked with the preparer's name and a permanent reference number, and every login, save, and permission change is permanently logged. This exists to protect honest work, not to create suspicion — but it means every action is traceable.

5. Consequences

Violations of this code — including using Nexus for personal ARN business, leaking client data, or copying proprietary methodology — may result in disciplinary action, termination, and legal proceedings as applicable under Indian law.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Nexus collects and how it is used, for both employees using the system and the clients whose data passes through it.

What Nexus collects

  • Employee account details: name, work email, role, and login activity
  • Client information entered by an advisor while using a tool: name, contact number, and the financial figures needed for that tool's calculation
  • Every saved report and the PDF generated from it
  • A permanent activity log: logins, reports saved, permission changes

How it's used

Client information is used solely to generate the financial reports and proposals an advisor is preparing for that client. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose outside servicing that client's relationship with Omega Financial.

Who can see what

An advisor can only see their own saved reports and the clients within them. Nexus administrators can see all reports and all activity logs across the firm, for oversight, audit, and business continuity purposes.

Data retention

Saved reports and activity logs are retained permanently and cannot be deleted by the advisor who created them — only a Nexus administrator can remove a report, and every deletion is itself logged.

Your acceptance

By using Nexus, every employee explicitly accepts the declaration presented at first login, confirming their understanding of client ownership, confidentiality, and the terms of this policy.

How to Use Nexus

Signing in

Use your registered email and password. If your organisation has two-factor login enabled, you'll also be asked to enter a one-time code sent to your email.

Finding a tool

From the Dashboard, click into a section (e.g. Sales or Customer Awareness) to see the tools available inside it. Click any tool to open it.

Saving a report

Every tool has a Save to Nexus button — this is the only way to produce a downloadable PDF. It locks the report permanently (only an admin can later edit or remove it), stamps it with your name and a reference number, and files it in that section's Reports page.

Finding a saved report

Open a section's Reports page (linked from the section screen) to browse everything saved there. Use the client name search box, or the tool picklist, to narrow results — the tool picklist can search across every section in Nexus, not just the one you're currently in.

For admins: managing the team

The Admin panel (visible only to admin accounts) lets you promote or block users, control which sections and tools each person can access, review every saved report, inspect the permanent audit log, and view the Analysis tab for proposal counts by user and time period.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I download a PDF directly from a calculator anymore?

PDFs are only available through Nexus's Reports section now, so that every report generated stays traceable, watermarked, and permanently on record — this protects both clients and advisors.

I made a mistake in a saved report. Can I fix it?

No — once saved, a report is locked and cannot be edited or deleted by the person who created it. This is by design. If a correction is genuinely needed, ask a Nexus admin, who can remove the report so you can save a corrected version.

Why do I need to enter a code from my email to sign in?

This is an additional security step to protect client data and confirm it's really you signing in, not just someone who has your password.

Can I access Nexus after I leave the company?

No. An admin can block any account instantly and completely — the moment that happens, that login can no longer read or save anything in Nexus, even if a browser session was still open.

Who can see the clients I've entered into a tool?

Only you and Nexus admins. Other advisors cannot see your saved reports or the clients in them.

What happens if I forget my password?

Use the "Forgot password?" link on the sign-in page to receive a reset link by email.
