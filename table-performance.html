1 · Select the Working Month

All weekly lotteries, daily reports and monthly winners are scoped to this month.

4 weeks are generated automatically the first time a month is loaded — lengths are randomised (7 or 8 days) but always add up to the full month, and stay fixed once created.

2 · Add / Update This Month's Roster — Name, Designation, Business Points

This is the reliable way to bring in the CRM report every month. Business Points is the evaluation criterion for Mentor suggestions and the Sales Champion award. One member per line: Name, Designation, Business Points (comma or tab separated — pasting from Excel works too). Loading this while a month is active also saves a snapshot for that month, so history and month-on-month comparisons stay accurate even as the roster changes later.

2b · Optional — Try Uploading the CRM PDF Directly

Best-effort only. Many CRM exports are saved as flattened images with no real text inside the PDF — in that case no tool can extract text from it, because there is nothing to extract. If you see "no rows matched" or "no selectable text," use the Quick Add box above instead.

Continuity — Export & Import Full Backup

This tool stores everything in this browser only. If you open a freshly re-downloaded copy of the file, or switch devices/browsers, the data will look empty even though nothing is "wrong" — it simply hasn't been restored yet. Export a backup whenever you finish working, and import it the next time you open the tool (even a new copy of the file) to continue exactly where you left off, with full month-on-month history intact.

Month-on-Month Continuity

When you load a previous month's roster via Quick Add (Setup tab, after switching the Month picker to that month), this tool automatically snapshots it. The Mentor-of-the-Month score for any month then automatically pulls the immediately preceding month's average Business Points for its month-on-month component — no manual re-entry needed.
